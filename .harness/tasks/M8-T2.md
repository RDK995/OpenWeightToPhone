Goal:
Add conversation deletion to the conversation store, and prove that a store constructed
FRESH from persisted storage alone recovers every conversation's full transcript intact,
lists them newest first, and does not resurrect a deleted conversation.

Tier: Cheap. Reason: none — bounded to one method plus tests, fully specified, verified
by a focused test command.

Relevant Requirements:
- FR3 — "A list of conversations, newest first, supporting create, open, and delete."
- FR3 — "The full transcript of each conversation is persisted locally on the phone."

Acceptance Criteria (milestone M8's AC1 and AC3, at unit level):
- `ConversationStore` gains `deleteConversation(id: string): void` on the exported
  interface and in the implementation. It removes the conversation and persists
  immediately.
- Deleting an id that does not exist: choose the behaviour that matches the file's
  existing house style and state which you chose in your Summary. Note the existing
  `requireConversation` throws `Conversation not found: <id>` for mutators, so throwing
  is consistent; a silent no-op is also defensible for a delete. Pick ONE, make it
  explicit, and cover it with a test. Do not leave it undefined.
- A deleted conversation is GONE AFTER REBUILD: a test must construct a SECOND,
  brand-new store instance over the SAME storage object (not the same store instance,
  and not an in-memory copy), and assert the deleted conversation is absent from both
  `loadConversations()` and `getConversation(id)`. This must prove deletion reached
  storage, not just the in-memory cache.
- FULL TRANSCRIPT RECOVERY: a test must build a conversation with several turns
  (including at least one `cancelled: true` turn and one multi-line/unicode content
  turn), then construct a brand-new store over the SAME storage and assert the recovered
  turns are deeply equal to what was written — same order, same count, same `role`,
  `content`, `cancelled` and `createdAt` on every turn. Assert on the whole turns array,
  not just its length.
- NEWEST FIRST SURVIVES A REBUILD: a test must assert `loadConversations()` from a
  freshly constructed store returns conversations ordered newest-first by `updatedAt`,
  with at least three conversations whose `updatedAt` values are distinct and whose
  insertion order is deliberately NOT the expected output order.
- Also assert `sessionId`, `profileId`, `title`, `createdAt` and a non-null `pending`
  record survive the rebuild intact.
